# Output Variants

Use the same captured work to generate different outputs without changing the core facts. When the source work is private, begin from the career-safe record, not from the raw internal evidence index.

## Internal evidence index

Use only inside the employer environment. It may contain private issue, PR, spec, dashboard, review, customer, metric, code, or screenshot references. Do not export, attach, publish, or place it in a personal directory.

## Career-safe version

Use for a personal career record, CV, LinkedIn, interviews, self-review, and compensation discussions.

Rules:
- remove private URLs, PR/issue numbers, code paths, schemas, query names, internal tool names, and configuration details
- remove customer, partner, employee, reviewer, and internal project names unless their use is authorized
- remove non-public metrics, incidents, roadmap information, and quotations
- keep the problem class, your role, material decision, tradeoff, result, and lesson
- state uncertainty honestly; do not convert delivery volume into business impact

## Interview version

Use when you need a spoken story.

Shape:
- context
- real difficulty
- your decisions
- tradeoff
- result
- what you learned

Keep it concrete and easy to say out loud.

## Resume bullet version

Use when you need concise bullets.

Rules:
- lead with the action you owned
- mention the product or system shape
- mention scope or technical surface
- include outcome or impact if known

## Self-review / promotion version

Use when the goal is to show growth, ownership, leverage, and seniority.

Emphasize:
- decision quality
- ambiguity handled
- team leverage
- cross-functional or domain impact
- what became reusable

## Public-writing version

Use when the work is private but you still want signal.

Rules:
- start from the career-safe version
- remove company-sensitive data and any remaining contextual clues
- remove exact numbers unless public and authorized
- generalize internal nouns
- keep the real lesson and the real decision

## Substack / portfolio angle

Use when the project has a portable lesson.

Possible angles:
- what made the work difficult
- what changed your mind
- what product judgment mattered most
- what AI made faster but not easier
