# Output Variants

Use the same captured work to generate different outputs without changing the core facts.

## Interview version

Use when you need a spoken story.

Shape:
- context
- real difficulty
- your decisions
- tradeoff
- result
- what you learned

Keep it concrete and easy to say out loud.

## Resume bullet version

Use when you need concise bullets.

Rules:
- lead with the action you owned
- mention the product or system shape
- mention scope or technical surface
- include outcome or impact if known

## Self-review / promotion version

Use when the goal is to show growth, ownership, leverage, and seniority.

Emphasize:
- decision quality
- ambiguity handled
- team leverage
- cross-functional or domain impact
- what became reusable

## Public-safe version

Use when the work is private but you still want signal.

Rules:
- remove company-sensitive data
- remove exact numbers if not safe
- generalize internal nouns
- keep the real lesson and the real decision

## Substack / portfolio angle

Use when the project has a portable lesson.

Possible angles:
- what made the work difficult
- what changed your mind
- what product judgment mattered most
- what AI made faster but not easier
