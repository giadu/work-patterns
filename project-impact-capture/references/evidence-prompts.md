# Evidence Prompts

Use these prompts when the story feels vague, inflated, or too implementation-heavy.

## Problem and importance

- What was broken, slow, unclear, or risky before this work?
- Who actually felt that problem?
- Why did this matter beyond "it was in the backlog"?

## Real difficulty

- What was the hardest part that was not just typing code?
- Was the main difficulty product, domain, rollout, AI quality, trust, operations, or coordination?
- What could have gone wrong if the wrong decision had been made?

## Your contribution

- What part was yours?
- What decision did you personally make?
- What direction would likely have happened without your input?

## Tradeoffs

- What did you intentionally keep out of scope?
- What future-friendly abstraction did you avoid for now?
- What did you optimize for in this version?

## Impact

- What became easier, faster, safer, clearer, or more valuable after shipping?
- Is there any visible evidence such as usage, stakeholder response, reduced support pain, or cleaner operations?
- If hard metrics do not exist yet, what qualitative evidence is still worth recording?

## Reusability

- What pattern from this work can be reused later?
- What would you do differently next time?
- Does this project support a stronger interview, promotion, or public story than you first thought?
