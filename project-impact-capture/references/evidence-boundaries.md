# Evidence Boundaries

Raw evidence makes career stories accurate, but private work artifacts are not automatically personal property. This reference keeps the skill useful without turning an employer's repositories, dashboards, or reviews into a personal archive.

## Employer-confidential evidence

Treat these as employer-only unless external use is explicitly authorized:

- private repository URLs, PRs, issues, comments, code, schemas, endpoint names, queries, logs, screenshots, credentials, and configuration
- customer, partner, employee, reviewer, or vendor identities
- internal usage, cost, performance, incident, security, financial, roadmap, or strategy data
- raw exports of GitHub, Slack, Notion, dashboards, or other internal systems

Use them to verify a story while access is legitimate. Keep them in the employer environment and do not copy them into a personal directory.

## Career-safe record

A career-safe record may keep the generalized problem class, your actual role, a material decision or tradeoff, broad technical scope, a qualitative result or risk reduction, and a lesson you can explain without reproducing the system.

## Final check before saving outside the employer environment

- Is every link either public or removed?
- Are all names, IDs, customer references, reviewer comments, and internal project names removed?
- Does the summary avoid code, schemas, architecture diagrams, configuration, internal metrics, and roadmap information?
- Does it distinguish what you owned from what the team owned?
- Would the statement still be defensible if the employer read it?

If any answer is no, keep the detail in the employer-only evidence index and rewrite the career-safe record.
