# Article Modes

Use these when the author has a topic but not a clear angle.

## Technical lesson

Explain one engineering insight from real work: the problem, what changed understanding, and what another engineer can do differently.

## Workflow or process insight

Focus on recurring friction, the system or workflow that changed it, and why that improved the work.

## AI in practice

Focus on what AI helped with, what still required judgment, and what made the workflow reliable rather than gimmicky.

## Systems note

Explore a pattern in software, teams, tools, habits, or organization. Ask what system sits underneath the visible symptom.

## Postmortem or what changed my mind

Show a shift in understanding: the original belief, the evidence that challenged it, and the revised view.

## Why this exists

Introduce a project, publication, or series: the need that created it, why it matters now, and what will follow.
